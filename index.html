How the Guessing Game Works

Our rhyming riddles turn reading into an exciting game of listening, thinking, guessing, pointing, and learning.

“I have a long trunk,
I’m big and gray,
I love to splash
and play all day.
Who am I?”

?
  1. 👂
    Listen

    Listen carefully to the rhyming clues.

  2. 💭
    Think

    Think about which animal it could be.

  3. ❓
    Guess

    Make your best guess.

  4. ☝️
    Point

    Find the animal in the illustration.

  5. 💡
    Learn

    Reveal the answer and learn something new.
