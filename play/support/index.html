Support

Help for parents using the It’s Guessing Time Android preview.

Install the Android preview

  1. On your Android device, choose Download for Android.
  2. Open the APK from your downloads and follow Android’s installation prompts. Android may ask you to allow installations from the app that opened the file.
  3. Open It’s Guessing Time from your home screen.

Unlocking worlds and songs

Each world starts with two free riddles. Solving an animal’s riddle unlocks that animal in My Discoveries and its song in Music Corner. Discover more animals to unlock bigger Memory Game challenges.

The downloadable preview does not take payments. Play Store purchases and Restore Purchases are planned for the Google Play version.

Contact support

Email miramilanbooks@gmail.com. Include your phone model, Android version, and what happened. Please leave out children’s names, photos, and other personal details.
