Privacy Policy

It’s Guessing Time Android preview and game website

Last updated November 16, 2026

Who we are

MiraMilan Books publishes It’s Guessing Time. Parents and guardians can reach us at miramilanbooks@gmail.com with privacy questions.

Progress and settings

The preview stores solved riddles and audio preferences on your device. The game uses that information to show discoveries, unlock songs and Memory Game challenges, and remember your preferences. The preview has no in-game account or payment form. You can remove the locally stored progress and preferences by clearing the app’s storage in Android settings.

The game website and APK download

These game pages use no analytics scripts, advertising scripts, or website accounts. The hosting provider may process standard connection and request information, such as IP addresses, to deliver and secure the site. The APK is served by GitHub; downloading it is subject to GitHub’s privacy statement.

Support and children’s privacy

The support email is for parents and guardians. If you contact us, we use your email address and the details you choose to share to respond and handle your request. We keep support correspondence as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable obligations. Please avoid sending children’s personal information. If you believe a child has shared personal information with us, or you want to ask about or request deletion of information you sent, contact us at the address above.

Future Google Play release

This policy covers the current preview and these game pages. We will update it before releasing a version that adds Google Play purchases or changes how the app handles data.
